I burst out onto the landing, leaving our front door wide open. The red cabinet on the wall opens easily. Inside it's empty, just a rolled-up fire hose with no nozzle and VANYA IS A LOSER written in marker. There's no extinguisher.

I run down to the eighth floor, then the seventh; that cabinet is empty too. Smoke is crawling down after me, out of our open door into the stairwell, and the stairwell sucks it upward like a chimney. The light on the landing goes off on its timer. I turn back.

Between the eighth and ninth floors the smoke is already thick, greasy and hot. It gets into my throat, I cough and can't stop coughing. I can't see the steps. I sit down on a step, then lie down with my cheek on it. It's eight steps to our door. I count them and can't finish counting.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing at the door of Varya's room again, and the handle won't give. A strip of orange light burns under the door.

I remember the empty red cabinet and VANYA IS A LOSER in marker. I remember eight steps in the dark that I never finished counting.

My throat is raw, though there's no smoke yet, only the smell. Behind me my wife is screaming my name.
