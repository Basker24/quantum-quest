I go around the slope from the road side, where it's gentler, and climb, tapping every step with the crowbar. The crust rings and holds. I move fast and do it right, like in a drill, and I'm glad I didn't run.

The wind is blowing off the river. It drives the smoke from the upper cracks straight onto this slope, onto me, and at first I hardly see it, just the smell, rotten, sweetish. Then my throat starts to burn as if I'd swallowed a box of match heads. My eyes water, and I rub them with my mitten.

Ten meters from Yegorka my knees give way. I sit down on the crust, warm as a stove, and think I'll sit for a minute. The crowbar slips out of my fingers and rolls down, clanging. Yegorka shouts something to me from above. My head goes heavy and calm, and I lie down with my cheek on the warm stone.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing by the fence with the "DANGER! BURNING SPOIL HEAP" sign again, and Vitka is yelling, "Yegor! Get down, I said!"

My throat is raw, like after a pack of Prima cigarettes on an empty stomach. I cough and spit, and my spit is gray.

I tested the crust properly, but the wind killed me. It blew off the river and drove all the smoke onto the slope I was climbing. I should have gone up against the wind, from the river side, where the smoke is carried away from the slope.
