I take hold of the beam, brace my boots against the floor and pull up. The beam won't move. I pull harder, the veins in my neck swell so much the mouthpiece slips out of my mouth, and I catch it with my teeth. The beam gives a finger's width, and the whole section above it sighs and settles back down, lower than before. Vitka screams into the rubber.

I pull again and again. Breathing through the self-rescuer gets harder and harder, I'm hot, my mouth tastes sweetish, like caramel. My arms don't give out all at once; first my fingers, then my hands. I sit down next to Vitka on the floor, just to rest, and he turns his head toward me.

His lamp shines at the roof, mine on his face. He says something, and I can't hear what. I feel good and sleepy, the way you do on the bottom bench in a banya. Vitka puts his hand on my shoulder, heavy, and I fall asleep under his hand.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing over Vitka by the crosscut again, and he spits out his mouthpiece and says, "Samokha. That you?"

The sweetness in my mouth is still there. I spit and spit, and it won't go away, caramel, carbon monoxide.

A beam you pull without support settles lower. My shoulders know that now. The hydraulic prop is lying a meter away from me, and a minute ago I didn't even notice it.
