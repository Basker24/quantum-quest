I jump into the cage with the second squad, and nobody stops me. Down at the shaft bottom it smells of burning and warm iron. The guys in masks go into the drift to longwall five, and I follow them without a mask, with just a self-rescuer, my collar pulled up over my nose. It's hot in the drift, like on the top bench of a banya, and hotter with every step.

About a hundred and forty meters in we find two men. One is sitting against the wall holding his head; the other is lying down. I take the one lying down under the arms. He's light; I drag him and think: there, it's working. I'll get him out.

Behind us, from the direction of the face, comes light. It's white and everywhere at once. I don't hear any sound. I'm lifted and thrown against the supports, and the air I breathe in burns inside me, all the way down to my stomach. My arms are still holding the man I was dragging, and then I stop feeling my arms, and him.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing by the cage again, and smoke and warm iron drift out of it.

Under my arms there's still somebody else's weight. I lower my arms, and they won't lower; they just hang there, bent. My face is burning from the inside, from the throat.

Down below, in three hours, it will blow a second time. I know it as surely as I know my own name. The guys from the second squad are checking their masks and joking about Palych's mother-in-law.
