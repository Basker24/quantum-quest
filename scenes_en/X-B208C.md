I go toward the child's voice, and the dark turns into water, black as the backwater. The boy is very close, above me, in the light; I can see his shorts and his white heels. I swim toward him, and he doesn't get any closer. The Choir, it turns out, is so big you can get lost in it.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm lying in the warm, crowded dark again, and outside the monitor is beeping wrong. Minute nine.

I swam after the boy and didn't catch him, though he stayed in one place the whole time. It must be too early for me to reach him.
