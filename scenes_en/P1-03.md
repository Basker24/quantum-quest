I'm eighty-three, though by the passport they made me I'm sixty-eight. At the market they call me Grandpa. People come to ask me whether to buy the garage, whether to go north, whether to marry this one. I answer briefly, charge little and almost never get it wrong. I myself sit at a plastic table under a beer umbrella behind the kiosk, drinking tea from a thermos.

::if F_П102 == КРОВЬ
In the freezer at my apartment, behind a pack of pelmeni, a thermos with two test tubes has lain for nine years. Once a year I open it and check that they're intact. Why I keep it I can't explain even to myself. Probably for the same reason a wolf buries a bone when it isn't hungry.
::end

::if F_П102 == ШАДРИН
Shadrin is in Moscow now; he has an office on the top floor and a laboratory in Novosibirsk. He sees me once a year, at his dacha, and every time asks one thing: when the boy from '96 has his birthday and where he lives. Every time I lie.
::end

::if F_П102 == СЖЁГ || F_П102 == ВЫЛИЛ
The blood they took from the boy in '96 burned long ago. Somewhere in Moscow one man in a white shirt remembers it, and Orpheus has stayed a foundation with a pretty business card. Those test tubes exist nowhere anymore.
::end

::if F_П102 == ПРОДАЛ
The blood from '96 has lain in Moscow with Orpheus for nine years. I don't think about it. I've learned not to think about what doesn't belong to me.
::end

In mid-June, a week after graduation, a young man comes up to my table. Tall, thin, crew-cut, in a new shirt his mother bought him for graduation and told him to take care of. On his right palm, when he scratches the back of his head, there's a white scar.

"They told me you give people work," he says, and blushes. "Any work. I need money; I want to go to Tomsk. Or not Tomsk. I haven't decided yet."

He doesn't recognize me. Why would he? I look at him, at myself, and see how many roads lie ahead of him, and he knows nothing about them yet. All these years I've held this market so that one day he'd come here. Only now do I understand that this was what I'd planned all along.
