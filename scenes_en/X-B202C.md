I unscrew the lid so the flask will stop hissing. The hissing does die down, but a white cloud tumbles out of the neck, dense as cotton wool, and falls onto my hands. At first my hands feel nothing, and then I feel nothing either, because the cloud fills the whole cabin.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing in the elevator between the second and third floors again. The bulb is flickering. The flask's lid is hissing.

I look at my hands, pink and warm. A minute ago they were white and not mine.

I just died, right here in this elevator. I stand repeating it to myself and can't stop.

The cloud from the neck fell to the floor and started rising, from my sneakers to my knees, from my knees to my face, like water in a bath when you forget to turn off the tap. The last thing I saw was the grille under the ceiling, dry, dusty, and the cloud hadn't reached it yet.
