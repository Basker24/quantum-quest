I pass the semi in the oncoming lane, blind, into the milk, and push it to a hundred and thirty. The red lights disappear from the mirror. For three minutes I drive alone through the white, and the white glows in the headlights like a screen.

Then right in front of me a gray wall grows out of the fog. A tarp across both lanes, wet, with the letters "…TRANS." I slam the brakes, and the wheel tears out of my hands. The blow comes in the chest. The belt cuts into my collarbone, and something inside me crunches, many times in a row, like footsteps on snow crust. I can't breathe in. In front of my face, wet canvas and an iron beam, with a drop hanging from the beam. The last thing I hear is the box from Kemerovo flying off the seat and banging against the glove compartment.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm driving through the fog again behind the semi's red lights, about fifty meters back, the needle at ninety.

I remember the gray wall and the drop on the beam. I remember the crunching inside, long, like footsteps on snow crust. The wheel is wet under my palms.

I take my foot off the gas, and the needle slowly creeps down.
