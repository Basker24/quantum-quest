I hit the door with my shoulder. Our door is pressed sawdust, but the frame is solid, and the chair on the other side holds. On the fourth blow the door splits by the handle. I shove my arm through the hole, feel for the back of the chair, and push it away.

The door opens, and the room breathes out at me. The fire comes out into the hallway all at once, like out of a stove when you open its door, right into my face. My hair crackles. I breathe in, because there's no way not to, and the air burns me from the inside, all the way down to my stomach.

I fall to the hallway floor and crawl toward where Varya was coughing behind the smoke. The skin on my hands is blistering. The carpet under my palms is hot and sticky. I crawl for as long as I still have hands.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing at the door of Varya's room again, and the handle won't give. A strip of orange light burns under the door.

I remember the room breathing fire into my face and me breathing that fire in. I run my palm over my face. My eyebrows are there, my skin is smooth, only wet with sweat.

I remember the hot, sticky carpet under my palms. Behind the door Varya is coughing, thin, a child's cough.
