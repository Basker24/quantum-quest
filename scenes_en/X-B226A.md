I go through the crowd, and the crowd suddenly surges toward the line, all at once, like coupled railcars when the locomotive jerks. I'm crushed chest-first against a metal barrier. My ribs crack, first one, then several at once. I can't breathe in; I can only stare at the white body armor right in front of my face. A streetlight is reflected in it. My sixty-six-year-old heart gives out before my ribs do.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing in the crowd outside the clinic again. The guards stand in a line; someone throws a snowball at the glass.

I touch my ribs through my coat and count them, checking they're all whole. I'm sixty-six, and I've gone and pushed in where you push in at sixteen.
