I head back toward his lamp. Seryoga is sitting by the drive, ripping the self-rescuer off his face and yelling that there's no air in it. I grab his hands, and he fights me off like a drunk in the drunk tank and elbows me in the mouthpiece. My self-rescuer flies out of my mouth.

It takes half a minute to find it on the floor, by feel. I do find it. But I've already breathed without it, twice, deep, like before a dive.

I get light-headed. Seryoga is still yelling, and I sit down next to him and think I ought to calm him down, say something boring, like Mikhalych. I open my mouth and can't remember a single word.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing at the face again. Seryoga's lamp is darting around the walls forty meters behind me.

My teeth remember his elbow. I touch my lips, and there's no blood; the mouthpiece is in place.

He wasn't suffocating in there. He only thought he was. And I sat next to him and couldn't find anything to say.
