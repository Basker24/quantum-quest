I come out of the willows into 2061, onto the dry riverbed, and walk home barefoot by memory, to the place where I lived. The streetlights burn on the wrong side of the street. On the corner where the dumpling shop used to be there's a pharmacy, and its sign is blue, though I remember green.

The building entrance is the same, but the door is different, steel, with a code I don't know. I wait for someone to come out. A woman in a housecoat comes out with a trash bag and looks at me the way people look at drunks. There's a light on in my window, and the curtains there are flowered.

"Who are you looking for, sir?"

I say my name.

::if F_ПАРТНЁР != НЕТ && F_ПАРТНЁР
Then hers.
::end

The woman shakes her head and walks around me. I sit down on the bench by the entrance, because my legs won't hold me anymore. Cold rises from my heels, slowly, like water in a basement. My chest gets tight, then empty. The last thing I see is the light going out in my window.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing in the willows again, wet to the waist, and the branches are still swaying behind me.

My heels are cold, as if I really had walked on asphalt. The fingers of my right hand won't straighten; the whole way I was clutching a key I don't have.

We never had flowered curtains. In sixty-five years the world has drifted so far that strangers live in my window, and I lost the key to that door long before tonight.
