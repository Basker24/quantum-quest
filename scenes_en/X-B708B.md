I grab the board, drop on my belly by the bank and crawl, pushing it ahead of me. The ice crunches but holds, because my whole body is lying on it. It's thirty meters to crawl. I count them in elbows, and at twelve Kolka's hand lets go of the edge.

He goes under quietly, without a scream. I drop the board and crawl faster, then get up on my knees, then on my feet. The ice by the hole sags under me like a mattress, and I go through right where he just was.

Under the water it's gray and murky. I feel around with my hands, find a hood, pull up, and we both hit the ice. The hole is somewhere off to the side. Kolka stops struggling before I do, and I hold on to him by the hood until I run out of air.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing by the fence again, shovel in my hands, and Kolka is holding on to the edge with one hand.

I remember counting elbows to twelve and his hand letting go of the edge. I look at his hand, and it's holding on, and another hand's width of the edge breaks off under it.

I remember the hood in my fist under the water. The board is lying by the fence, long and reliable, and it's thirty meters to the hole.
