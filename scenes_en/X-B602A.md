"I'm here!" I shout into the dark. "Hold on, you hear? I'm here!"

And he hears me. The shouting breaks off, and in the silence someone very close, right at my ear, says in surprise, "You?" Then a weight settles on my chest.

At first the weight is just warm, like a cat. Then it becomes a sack, then a mine cart. My ribs crack; I hear the crack with my ears. I'm lying on a dry hospital sheet, and something fine, cold and loose is packing into my mouth, my nose, behind my teeth. I can't see it, the ward is dark, but I know that taste: Dad's hands smell like that when he comes home from a shift. I cough, and there's nothing to cough with; everything's packed full.

I kick at the foot of the bed. Somewhere far off Semyonych rolls over and mutters about fare dodgers. Roma sits up in bed and watches. He doesn't shout; he doesn't talk at all.

The last thing I feel is Mom's hair as she leans over me before school, coal dust from the processing plant. Only here there's so much dust there's nothing left to breathe.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm lying on the bed again, on my back, and in the dark someone is wheezing, "Guys! Anybody there?"

I spit into my palm. The spit is clear, ordinary. I spit again and again, until my mouth goes dry, and still I feel sand on my teeth, fine as flour. My ribs hurt one by one; I could count them.

I answered him, and he took me in with him. On the pillow under my cheek it was black and wet; that I remember for sure. Roma saw. Roma is sitting up in bed now too, looking at me, and I don't know how much of it he saw this time.
