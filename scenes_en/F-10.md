I come out of the willows into 2061, onto the dry riverbed, into the fog before dawn. There's no Choir in me. Only at the very bottom, where the chill sat all my life, something holds on by a thin thread, like a draft from under a door.

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ВНУК
Andryusha is asleep on the stone in the plaid blanket, holding his fishing rod tight. I pick him up and carry him home. He's heavy, warm, and smells of the river.
::end

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ПАРТНЁР
The one who was waiting for me on the stone gets up to meet me and asks nothing. We walk home across the dry riverbed, and I hold her hand the way I held it the first time.
::end

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ДОЧЬ
Mali is asleep on the stone with a cold thermos. I wake her, and she says in Russian, with an accent, "Papa, I'm cold." We go home.
::end

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ДАША
Dasha is sitting on the stone writing in her notebook. Seeing me, she finishes the line, closes the notebook and walks beside me in silence.
::end

For five years the thread is silent. I live like any old man: the clinic, the market, the bench by the entrance. Sometimes I forget that I once heard neighboring worlds, and those are the best days.

In February '66, at four in the morning, I wake from a cold at the back of my head. I haven't had a cold like that since '61. I get up and go to the kitchen barefoot, and there's a smell of gas, very faint, so faint nobody would notice in the daytime. A burner is open a quarter turn, with no flame.

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ВНУК
Andryusha is sleeping over at our place, on a camp bed behind the wall. He's twelve.
::end

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ПАРТНЁР || F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ДОЧЬ || F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ДАША
Behind the wall sleeps the person I held on to that thread for.
::end

I close the burner and open the window vent. Frosty air comes into the kitchen like water through a hole in a hull. I stand at the window until it begins to get light, and I feel the thread inside me break, quietly, without pain, the way a cobweb breaks when you walk through it.

The chill never came again. I needed it exactly once.
