The first two kilometers I walk briskly and even sing Splean's "No Way Out" to myself. By the third I can't feel my toes. In Lagerny Garden the bench by the monument is all frost and for some reason looks warm. I sit down for a minute to catch my breath. The stars over the Tom are big, and I don't feel like getting up anymore.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing outside Lyokha's entrance again. Forty-two below. Streetlights in rust-colored fog.

I'm hot, and my feet feel like somebody else's, though I haven't taken a step yet. The worst of it is that the bench was warm.
