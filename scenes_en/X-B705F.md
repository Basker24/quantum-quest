The first KAMAZ roars past, the Granta rocks, and I pull out from behind the semi just as I planned, right behind it. The oncoming lane is empty, I know it is. I floor it and don't even look ahead; I look at the trailer, at how much of it is left.

Halfway along the trailer two more headlights come out of the white, high up, at the height of my head. A rust-orange KAMAZ, just like the first. In the life I remembered, it wasn't there.

I don't have time to jerk the wheel. The last thing I see is the little icon on its dashboard, the same as the first one's.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm driving behind the semi on the straight before Yurga again, and the wipers are chasing snow dust across the glass.

I remember two pairs of headlights, one after the other. The first I knew by heart. Nobody knew the second.

I remember the icon on the dashboard, two identical icons. Varya asks, "Dad, are we gonna pass it?" I look at the tarp with the dumplings on it and understand there's nothing left to remember here.
