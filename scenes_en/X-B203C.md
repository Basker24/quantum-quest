I say something to Lena about the cold and how it's time to go. She says, "Well, go on, then." What that means, I don't understand, and I walk. On along the same path through Lagerny Garden, until I can't feel my toes, all the way to the bench by the monument.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing outside Lyokha's entrance again. Lena is picking at the frost on the railing with her mitten.

She was asking me to stay. It hits me now as clearly as if she'd said it out loud. And that me didn't understand and walked off to freeze.
