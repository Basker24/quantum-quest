I stay. The voices get louder, then quieter, and outside there's no more beeping. There's a steady tone, like the dial tone when somebody's hung up on the other end. Lunyov is pressing on my chest with both hands, counting out loud. I recognize that counting, only this time it doesn't reach two hundred and fifty-two.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm lying in the warm, crowded dark again, and outside the monitor is beeping wrong. Minute nine.

I remember the steady tone. I remember Lunyov stopping counting. The cold now is the same, and the voices are calling the same way. Hearing it all once was enough for me.
