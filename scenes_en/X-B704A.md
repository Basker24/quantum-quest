I get up and push the damper shut all the way. The heat right away turns even and soft, better than before. My father-in-law lies down on the lower bench and says he'll just lie there a minute. I sit down next to him.

My head gets heavy, like after two shifts back to back, and my temples pound, but it feels so good I don't want to move. I think I'll get up in a moment and go out. Then it turns out I've been lying on the floor for a long time, cheek on the wet boards, and the boards are warm. I throw up right on them, and I don't care.

My father-in-law is snoring. Then the snoring stops in the middle. I want to tell him he's stopped snoring, and I say it somewhere inside my head, but my mouth doesn't open.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm sitting on the bench in the steam room again, and my father-in-law is waving at the flue: "Shut the damper, Andryukha."

I remember the warm wet boards under my cheek. I run my palm over my face, and it's dry and hot, no boards.

I remember the snoring stopping in the middle. That stopping makes me sicker than the heat does. Little blue flames run over the coals.
