The water in the backwater grows light. Under the black night surface, as under glass, lies a sunny July noon. Sixty-five years have met here edge to edge, like two pieces of canvas, and the seam runs right along the water. I see the sandy bottom and the white snag. From above, sixty-five years away, a girl's voice starts to count.
