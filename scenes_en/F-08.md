He rolls up his sleeve without asking why. He's eighteen, and I'm Grandpa, whom the whole market obeys. A nurse from the clinic on Vesennyaya Street, whom I pay to keep quiet, takes his blood. The test tube is warm; I hold it in my palm until it cools.

::if F_П102 == ШАДРИН
I take the test tube to Shadrin's dacha myself. He holds it up to the window for a long time and says, "So you did know where he lives after all." A month later more money arrives in my account than I made at the market in ten years. A year later Shadrin opens a clinic in Novosibirsk.
::end

::if F_П102 == КРОВЬ
I buy a laboratory in Novosibirsk, in Akademgorodok, along with people who haven't been paid for two years. They're as glad of three test tubes as children. Six years later I have what in our world wasn't made until the forties: an injection after which a person wakes up once, a minute before his death.
::end

The "second chance" appears twenty years earlier than it should have. The ad hangs at the bus stop on Sovetsky when the boy whose blood the nurse took behind my kiosk isn't yet thirty. He stands at the bus stop looking at it, and the scar on his palm, I know, is aching.

::if F_П102 == ШАДРИН
The Wager begins in '27. People who've bought themselves one extra life stop fearing war before they've learned to fear it. Shadrin is seventy-nine then, and they say he chooses himself whom to inject and whom not.
::end

::if F_П102 == КРОВЬ
The Wager begins in '27. The first to buy my injection are generals. I sit in my office with windows on the Ob and watch the news show people getting up after being hit and walking forward again. I'm paid so much that money stops being money.
::end

I die in '31; my body is a hundred and seven, though no one but me knows it. They give me the injection an hour before death; I ordered it myself. At four-twelve I die and wake at four-eleven, in the same ward, a minute before my death, with a mouth full of a stranger's Choir. His minute now sounds in everyone who bought the injection, and all of them, in my voices, beg for one more.

The second death comes at four-twelve. There's no rewind after it.

! Ending "My Own Orpheus." You sold his blood before the others did. My Life shows what other roads led to this morning.
