In Yurga I pull into the gas station and buy coffee in a paper cup, the way Varya would. I stand with it by the pump, outside, because it's stuffy in the car. The coffee is hot, and I blow on it.

Behind me a Niva revs. The driver's hung over; he can't see me behind the pump and backs up hard to swing around toward the exit. The tow hitch hits me behind the knees, I fall backward, and the Niva rolls over me with a rear wheel, and then, panicking, forward again.

The coffee runs across the asphalt, steaming. I lie on my back and see the gas station canopy over me, white lamps still on from the night. Somebody is shouting from the cashier's window.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing at the pump in Yurga again with a paper cup of coffee, and it's still too hot.

I remember the tow hitch behind my knees and the wheel going over me twice, back and forth. I remember the white lamps overhead.

The Niva is sitting by the exit; the driver is just starting the engine. I step behind the pump, up against the cashier's wall, and watch him back up to where I was just standing and drive off without noticing. A minute from now Varya will be standing here.
