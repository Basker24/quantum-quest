I dive in headfirst, like a boy, only I haven't been seven for a long time. The cold hits my chest like a fist, and my old heart stumbles and stops. I see the boy very close, in the sunlit water, his outstretched hand. I can't reach out my own.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing on the stone by the water again. The backwater is growing light, the boy is sinking by the snag, a girl's voice is counting from above.

My heart is racing like a push-started engine. I remember the cold in my chest and the child's hand I couldn't reach. I'm seventy-two; I can't go headfirst anymore, and the boy is down there by the snag, and the girl is already counting.
