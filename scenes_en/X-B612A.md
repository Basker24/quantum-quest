I walk across the crust, fast, without stopping. Underfoot it crunches like frozen snow.

::if F_В611 == ПЛОТ
The raft drags behind me on its rope, and behind the raft the crust bursts, hole after hole, a black trail. The rope cuts into my shoulder. The barrels boom.
::end

At two hundred meters the crust goes out from under my feet all at once, softly, without a crack. I sink to my waist in something warm. The sludge is hot, thick, and pulls me down gently, like a feather bed. Bubbles burst above it, and the smell of rotten eggs is so strong my eyes water.

I grab the edges of the hole, and the edges break off in my hands like wafer. I breathe through my mouth, fast. Then suddenly the smell is gone. Completely, as if someone had switched it off, and I have time to be glad, because it's easier to breathe.

My head becomes light and large. I sit chest-deep in the sludge looking at the pipe over the field; it swims and doubles. I want to call someone and can't remember how you call people. The last thing is the smell of Mom, coal dust from her hair, though nothing can smell of anything anymore.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing at the edge of the settling ponds again, and the crust lies before me, gray and quiet.

My legs are hot up to the waist, as if I'd just come out of a bathhouse, and I feel my trousers: dry, dusty. My head is humming. There's a rotten smell in my nose, and then it's gone, and for some reason I pinch my nose and let go, checking.

Out there on the crust the smell disappeared too, and I was glad. That's what I remember best.

::if F_В611 == ПЛОТ
At the end I remember one more thing: the rope on my shoulder. Behind me the crust kept bursting under the barrels, hole after hole, and the last hole went under my feet. It was the raft. I lashed it together in Mezhdurechensk, by the fuel depot, over three days, and Granny Nyura said, "You'll drown."

My shoulder aches under a rope that isn't on it. I rub it and think about that May morning by the fuel depot, the rusty sides of the barrels, and I can't stop.
::end
