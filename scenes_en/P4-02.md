At night I walk to the backwater and sit on the bank, on that very flat stone Vitka jumped from this morning. Grasshoppers creak in the bushes. The water lies black and level, and the snag at the far edge shows white in the dark, like a hand someone raised and forgot to lower.

::if F_НА_БЕРЕГУ == ВНУК
All evening I think about Andryusha. He's there, in 2061, on the stone, alone. They'll collect him in five minutes; I made sure of that myself, and they'll take him home. I know that as surely as I know he'll forgive me later. Not right away.
::end

Before it's light a man in a suit comes down to the water. He has no fishing rod; he just stands at the edge looking at the water. Then he takes off his jacket, folds it on a stone and squats beside it, the way people squat by a grave.

::if F_ЗНАЕТ_ДЕДА
I recognize him. It's Vadim Petrovich Lunyov, Granddad's graduate student, forty years old. This afternoon at City Hospital No. 2 he took two test tubes of blood from a boy and told the boy's mother he was a doctor from Tomsk.
::end

::if !F_ЗНАЕТ_ДЕДА
I don't recognize him right away. It's the doctor from Tomsk who in '96 stood over my hospital bed with two test tubes and smiled at Mom. This afternoon that's exactly where he was standing. What he's doing here at four in the morning, I don't know.
::end

::if F_МИТЯ
I know why he's here. In '88 his son, Mitya, drowned in this backwater. They never found the body. Every July Lunyov comes and sits here until morning.
::end

He doesn't see me yet. I'm sitting twenty steps away, on a stone, barefoot, in my dried striped shirt. Over Krasnaya Gorka the edge of the sky is beginning to gray.
