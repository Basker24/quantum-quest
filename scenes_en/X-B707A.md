I undo two screws, and the outlet falls out of the wall into my palm, hanging on its wires. I don't check the live wire with the tester; no time. With my left hand I hold on to the radiator under the window so I won't fall over from my crouch, and with my right I take hold of the wires to pull them out longer.

My right arm cramps at once, all the way to the shoulder. My fingers clench on the wire by themselves, and I can't open them, though I try very hard. The current goes through me into the radiator. Something in my chest jerks like a fish on a line, once, twice, and my heart misses a beat and never gets back in step.

I'm lying on the floor by Varya's desk, my cheek on her slipper with bunny ears. The screwdriver rolls under the bed. Varya is screaming on the bed, thin and long, and I look at the slipper.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm crouching by Varya's desk again, the screwdriver in my hand, and the outlet is dangling from the wall.

I remember my fingers clenching on the wire by themselves and not letting go. I put the screwdriver down on the floor, farther away, and wipe my palm on my pants, though it's dry.

I remember the bunny-eared slipper right next to my face, and Varya screaming. Right now she's sitting on the bed with her feet tucked under her, watching me like a magician.
