I run up, straight at him, to where Yegorka is standing. The crust crunches under my boots like snow crust in March. Heat comes up through my soles, and after ten steps I can't feel my toes.

At the twentieth step the crust under me rings differently, dull, and I have time to hear it. My right leg goes down to the knee, all at once, without a crack, as if I'd stepped into a snowdrift. Below it's empty and bright, orange. My pant leg flares up, my boot runs.

I yank my leg, and the crust around it breaks in a circle, like ice around a hole. I fall in chest first. The heat comes into my mouth, and I stop breathing before I understand I'm burning. The last thing I hear is Vitka shouting "Yegor!" down below.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm standing by the fence with the "DANGER! BURNING WASTE DUMP" sign again, and Vitka is yelling, "Yegor! Get down, I said!"

My right leg is baking from the inside, from knee to heel. I look at my boot and wait for it to run.

On that slope, where steam comes out of the cracks, the crust is hollow, and it's bright underneath. Where nothing grows, it rings dull. I heard that a second before I went through.
