I'm in the pulmonology ward three times in a year, then five times the next. Nika comes to me last on her rounds, when the others are already asleep, sits on the edge of the bed and is silent. We have plenty to talk about, but I don't have the air for talking.

Anthracosis, the dust. In thirty-six years I've breathed a second seam into my lungs, and it has settled there evenly, in layers, the way coal settles in the earth. The morning cough lasts half an hour now, then an hour, and then I stop counting.

You can rewind away from a collapse. From an outburst, from fire, from black water in a corridor too; there death has a minute, and in that minute I learned to come back. Dust has no such minute. It built up breath by breath, nine thousand shifts of six hours, and not one of those breaths can be played again. Granddad in his log would probably have called it something scientific. I call it the Wall.

Since '47 they've given me oxygen at home. The machine hums by the bed as steadily as the ventilation at the longwall, and I sleep better to that hum than to silence. At four-twelve I wake and go out onto the balcony with the tube in my nose, the way my father went out to smoke.

::if F_МИТЯ
The sandal marked "Lunyov D." stands in my china cabinet, behind the glass, wrapped in cellophane. I never found anyone to give it to. The graduate student Lunyov, if he's alive, is a very old man, and I don't know where he is.
::end

In the winter of '49, at dawn, my heart gives out before my lungs. I'm sitting on the balcony, on a stool, looking at the spoil heap of the Glubokaya, white with snow. The Choir at the back of my head hums quietly and evenly, like the transformer in the yard. None of them calls me back, because here there's nowhere to go back to.

Afterward someone sets my lamp on the railing, its number toward the street. Who set it there, I can no longer see.

! Dead end "Black Lungs." The gift doesn't save you from what comes slowly. This you never reached the Black Backwater. My Life shows where else you could have turned.
