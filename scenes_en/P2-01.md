I choose 2061, and the Choir starts to leave.

First the distant ones fall silent, the ones I hardly remember. Then the near ones. The voices go out one at a time, like the windows in the building across the street when everyone goes to bed. I stand in the willows, holding a branch, and listen to it getting quiet inside me. For sixty-five years it was never quiet in there for a minute.

::if F_ПУТЬ == ЛАМПА
The last to go is the one who stayed under the rubble at the Glubokaya. He tells me what he always told me at the face: "Don't take off your self-rescuer."
::end

::if F_ПУТЬ == ПРОТОКОЛ
The last to go is the one who died in the Tomsk lab, in the chair with the wires. He says nothing, just clicks his tongue, like a relay.
::end

::if F_ПУТЬ == ПАРТИЯ
The last to go is the one the storm killed on the bald peak in Shoria. He smells of ozone and wet rock.
::end

::if F_ПУТЬ == БАРАБАН
The last to go is the one who stayed in the basement, on the green felt, with the cylinder at his temple. He laughs quietly, the way you laugh at a good joke.
::end

::if F_ПУТЬ == ТЁПЛАЯ_ВОДА
The last to go is the one who drowned in the warm sea. He says one word in Thai that I never learned.
::end

::if F_ПУТЬ == ХОР
The last to go are all of them at once, because all my life I heard them equally loud. The hermitage, the hospital, the road. They leave in chorus, the way they sang.
::end

::if F_ПУТЬ == СЕМЬЯ
The last to go is the one who once didn't get to his family in time. He says, "At least you make it."
::end

One thin thread remains, at the very bottom, where the chill sat all my life. That one I can still hold on to, if I want. And beyond the bushes the boy on the sand is coughing and crying, and the girl is stroking his wet head. From here I can still say one more word to him; the seam is holding for now.
