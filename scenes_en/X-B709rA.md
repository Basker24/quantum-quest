I pull into the oncoming lane behind the semi without looking, the way Varya would if she were in a hurry. Far back in the mirror her red Lada bobs along. The semi beside me is long and wet, spray from it hits the windshield, and the wipers can't keep up.

The oncoming car comes over the rise with no headlights. Small, dark, almost invisible in the morning drizzle. I see it when it's a semi's length away, and I only have time to think that a minute from now Varya would see it just like this.

It hits my left headlight. The car spins and is thrown under the semi's rear wheel. The wheel goes over the hood, then over me. It smells of wet asphalt and gasoline.

## Пробуждение
…and I'm driving behind the semi on the straight before Yurga again, and the wipers are chasing spray across the glass.

I remember the small dark car with no headlights, coming over the rise in the oncoming lane. I remember the semi's wheel going over me, and having time to think about Varya.

So that's what's waiting for her here, a minute from now. I slow down and stay behind the semi. My hands on the wheel are wet. For the first time I died on purpose, and I woke up pleased.
